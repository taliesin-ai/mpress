# B02 — accessibility panel reachability, 2026-10-01

Beads `mp-b52.2.2`, confirmed P1 bug. This fixes existing settings; no controls or dependencies were added.

## Defects and change

Production CSS requires a 400px body minimum and enables scrolling on both the body and phone panel. The bounded panel cannot fit its header, tabs, body and footer in a short window. Enlarged text worsens the clipping. An additional 761×601 check, just outside the compact breakpoint, proves that retaining the minimum there leaves the last colour option unreachable.

The body minimum is now zero, allowing the desktop grid to shrink its scrolling content. At widths up to 760px or heights up to 600px, all four panel rows use their content height and the panel owns scrolling. The body stops scrolling independently there. Scroll padding keeps keyboard focus clear of clipping edges; overscroll containment preserves the underlying page position. The desktop grid, settings, saved preferences and site-specific contribution scripts remain supported.

The old unit test explicitly required the defective 400px CSS literal. That assertion was removed, while its viewport/grid budget checks remain. Generated-production browser checks now exercise the actual reachability contract.
